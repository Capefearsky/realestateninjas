Common questions

Does it cost anything to get matched?

No. Matching is free for buyers and sellers. Partner agents pay a referral fee only when a deal closes.

How do you choose my agent?

We look at local agents’ recent closed sales, response times and the neighborhoods and price ranges they know best, then pick one agent who fits your move.

Am I obligated to work with the agent?

No. You’re free to say no, and you can ask for a different match before you sign anything with an agent.

What areas do you cover?

We’re launching first in North Carolina. Join the waitlist and we’ll tell you when matching opens in your area.

Will an agent contact me now?

No. Until launch we only send launch updates. We never sell your information.
